In a real-world scenario, you would need to generate proper icons for your PWA.
For this project, you need to generate the following icon sizes:
- icon-72x72.png
- icon-96x96.png
- icon-128x128.png
- icon-144x144.png
- icon-152x152.png
- icon-192x192.png
- icon-384x384.png
- icon-512x512.png
- maskable_icon.png (with safe area to ensure proper display in all formats)

You can use tools like:
- https://maskable.app/editor to create maskable icons
- https://realfavicongenerator.net/ to generate full icon sets
- https://app-manifest.firebaseapp.com/ to generate manifest and icons
